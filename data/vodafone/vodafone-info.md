Today our [Vodafone live!cast](vodafone-live-cast) project was also announced in [Vodafone Portugal](https://www.vodafone.pt): *“Vodafone lança informação gratuita em rodapé no ecrã do telemóvel (21 Junho 2006)“*.

> Vodafone Ticker (previously known as “live!cast” (…)) was announced last year (in Germany), and is probably inside your own device, but not yet enabled by your operator. It was the next big thing, the “RSS for mobiles” some said.

Vodafone Info provides users with real-time and personalized information like sports headlines, weather updates, and breaking news headlines to your mobile phone’s display.

This new service will provide users with real-time and personalized information. For example, sports fans can receive updates on their favorite teams, while news junkies can stay up-to-date on the latest headlines. Weather updates will also be available, so users can always be prepared for the day.
