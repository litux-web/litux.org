Another product launch from our team. Now on Vodafone Portugal stores, soon in other countries.

A big thank you for all teams at [Vodafone Portugal](https://www.vodafone.pt), [Huawei](https://www.huawei.com) and [Wit Software](https://www.wit-software.com).

The Vodafone 520 Photo Frame is a digital photo frame that was launched in 2007. It was one of the first digital photo frames to feature a cellular modem, which allowed users to receive photos over the air.

The Vodafone 520 Photo Frame was a popular product, and it was sold in a number of countries around the world. However, it was discontinued in 2009, as the market for digital photo frames began to decline.

Despite being discontinued, the Vodafone 520 Photo Frame remains a popular collector's item. It is a reminder of the early days of digital photography, and it is a testament to the innovative spirit of Vodafone.

Here are some of the technical specifications of the Vodafone 520 Photo Frame:

* Display: 5.2-inch TFT LCD
* Resolution: 320 x 240 pixels
* Storage: 128MB internal memory, expandable via microSD card
* Connectivity: 3G

The Vodafone 520 Photo Frame was a groundbreaking product that helped to usher in the era of digital photography. It was a popular product, and it is still a sought-after collector's item today.
